The research

Recover the structure behind the image.

Image-to-markup generation

The 2016 work, What You Get Is What You See: A Visual Markup Decompiler, studies learning to reconstruct presentational markup from rendered images. It demonstrates a shared neural approach on mathematical expressions in LaTeX and synthetic web pages in HTML.

The project connects visual markup decompilation with model design, datasets, and evaluation. Read the original paper.

Coarse-to-fine attention

The ICML 2017 paper develops an attention-based encoder–decoder for image-to-LaTeX generation. A coarse-to-fine attention mechanism selects a support region before attending at a finer scale, reducing the cost of visual attention.

The study evaluates printed expressions and transfer to handwritten expressions. Read the published paper.

The dataset

IM2LATEX-100K

Mathematical expression images paired with their LaTeX source, introduced in the image-to-markup research of Yuntian Deng and collaborators.

The formulas originate in scientific articles from arXiv, distributed through the Cornell KDD Cup source collection. The dataset pairs rendered formulas with markup for training and evaluating mathematical expression recognition.

Our dataset page brings together the original archive, the hosted raw and processed files, and the Hugging Face exports, with the distinctions needed for reproducible comparisons.
